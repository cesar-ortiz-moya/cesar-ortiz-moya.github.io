About me

Academic Background

I am a Visiting Assistant Professor of Economics at Southern Illinois University Edwardsville (SIUE) and a PhD Candidate in Economics at the University of Málaga, Spain.

My dissertation, The Economics of Space Debris: Mitigation Policies, Abatement Costs, and Economic Impact , is supervised by Professor Anelí Bongers , with Professor José Luis Torres serving as my doctoral tutor.

In 2025, I was a Visiting Research Fellow at the University of Colorado Boulder, hosted by Professor Daniel Kaffine .

My research combines dynamic macroeconomic modelling, environmental economics, and quantitative policy analysis to investigate the economic implications of orbital infrastructure and space debris.
